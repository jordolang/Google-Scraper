## 🎯 Marketing Strategy & Psychology

### Why This Works:

1. **Exclusivity Creates Value**
   - "Email only" makes recipients feel special
   - Creates sense of insider access
   - Not available on website = scarcity

2. **Two-Tier Approach**
   - Everyone gets something ($99 off)
   - Budget-conscious get more ($149 off)
   - Creates choice without pressure

3. **Win-Win Positioning**
   - Attribution framed as mutual benefit
   - Not just "give us free advertising"
   - "Help us grow while you save"

4. **Clear Transparency**
   - Exactly what attribution includes
   - Exactly what it doesn't affect
   - Builds trust through honesty

5. **Flexible & Removable**
   - Can be removed later
   - Reduces buyer hesitation
   - Shows confidence in service

### When Discussing Attribution Option ($350):

## 🔒 Legal & Ethical Considerations

### Attribution Content Guidelines:

**MUST:**
- Be truthful and accurate
- Focus on development services
- Respect client's brand
- Be professionally presented
- Be clearly identified as attribution

**MUST NOT:**
- Make false claims about client's business
- Misrepresent client pricing/offers
- Create confusion about services
- Compete with client's offerings
- Mislead visitors in any way

### Contract Language:

Include clear terms about:
- Exact placement of attribution
- Content of attribution pages
- Client's right to remove
- Price adjustment if removed
- Timeline for removal requests
