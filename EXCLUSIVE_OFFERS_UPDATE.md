## 🎯 Marketing Strategy

### Why Two Tiers?

**Tier 1 ($400) - Everyone Wins:**
- Makes ALL email recipients feel special
- Significant discount (20%) without conditions
- Instant gratification
- Removes price objection

**Tier 2 ($350) - Budget-Conscious Win More:**
- Additional savings for those who need it
- Provides portfolio building opportunity
- Win-win framing (not just "free advertising")
- Optional = no pressure

### Psychology Behind the Approach:

1. **Exclusivity** - "Email only" creates scarcity and special treatment
2. **Transparency** - Clear explanation builds trust
3. **Flexibility** - "Removable" reduces hesitation
4. **Choice** - Two options empower the customer
5. **Value Stacking** - $99 + optional $50 = compelling offer
